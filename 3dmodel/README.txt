Wemo mini with 1.3 oled snap case by jxoco on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6667237

Summary:
For Weather station,Octoprint monitor or your own project.Small and simple it holds the Wemos D1 mini and the 1.3 OLED screen.The case snaps together.Tinkered the single display of the remix item to add the internal holder for the Wemos and to get it to snap together. And I had to move the hole in the side.You can see in the pictures that to hold the screen in place, I put a small piece of the foam that comes on the pins of an ESP8266 or ESP32 to protect the pins in shipping.The USB connector end of the Wemos slides under a lip to secure it.The opposite side of the Wemos is held by a little bulge on the TopC version.So, the wemos can be completely snapped in.If you wanted to, you could use a soldering iron to melt over the pins that the OLED sits on to more permanently attach the OLED.update * I had to rename the STL file for the tighter Top.TopC file snaps in a bit tighter.**** I have updated this, the newer version is;https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6686649
